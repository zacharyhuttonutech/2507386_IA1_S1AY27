The Richest Man in Babylon

i read “The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse” and “The Five Laws of Gold” from George S. Clason’s book. Arkad is the person telling the story of how he got rich and the rest of Babylon wanted the steps not a mystery. I am a student so Im not sitting on a chest of gold. I get money sometime and I spend it fast if I do not have rules. That is the part I can use.

Jump to the seven cures or the five laws.

Book: The Richest Man in Babylon on Project Gutenberg

Seven cures for a lean purse

1. Start thy purse to fattening

Arkad's first rule is to set aside atleast ten percent of your income before you spend anything else. His point isn't that one lucky week will make you wealthy. He means your pouch stays empty if every coin you earn is spent right away. When I get income, it doesn't come from a regular paycheck, whenever I get money it already has a purpose, lunch, buying credit, or taxi fare. Ten percent of almost nothing feels like a waste for me. This chapter challenged my excuse, it's the habit that fixes things, not the size of the amount. So as soon as cash comes to me I can save a tenth of it, even if it's just a tiny bit kept apart in my wallet. If I hold off till the weekend there's going to be nothing left to put away, and I just keep telling myself I'll start once I get work. That's just my broke wallet agreeing with my excuses.

2. Control thy expenditures

The second cure is the one that bothers me because I already know it and I still do the opposite. Arkad says what each of us calls our necessary expenses will grow to match what we earn unless we say no to some of them. The budget he wants is needs first then whats left. My version of needs is data so I can submit work away from home, and transport money so I actually reach Papine every day. After that I start calling snacks and random online stuff “small.” Then over time they are the reason the tenth never happens. So the alignment is a boring list for me: school travel and data come before the extra. If the extra is not in the nine-tenths then it waits. I dont need a finance app for that, I just need to stop pretending every want is a need because I thought of it in the line at the shop or somewhere

3. Make thy gold multiply

Once there is a saved tenth, Arkad says it has to work. Gold that sits still is not the point of the chapter. Hes saying he wants it earning more gold. Currently i do not have a business to put my money into, and my savings tin is not an investment fund. Time spent on doing stuff that makes little money is not in vain, its me making the little I have multiply later. If I only save and never learn then I am protecting a small amount and staying stuck.

4. Guard thy treasures from loss

This one is about not handing gold to a plan you do not understand, and not about taking advice from someone who is loud but has never done the thing either. Arkad’s people lose money because the story sounded quick adn easy, I see the same story play out in live society, easy cash, day trading, lotto scamming etc.. I dont have wealth to lose which makes it easier to think the warning is for older people but Its not. A bad spend when you are a student sets you back further because you cannot earn it again tomorrow. My guard is simple now, If I cannot explain how the money comes back after i spend it I do not put it in. School is the safer bet I already understand a little: show up, do the courses, finish the degree. That is slow and slow is the point of this cure.

5. Make of thy dwelling a profitable investment

Arkad wants men to own their homes so the rent is not a permanent leak. I am not buying a house this year or any time soon so reading the chapter and then writing “I will buy property” wouldnt make sense What I can do now though is make the place I already study worth something, my home. A corner that is only for school work, charger in the same spot, phone not in my hand the whole time so i dont get distracted. That corner does not pay rentriught now but It stops me losing the hours I already paid for with fees and transport that were given to me SO when i ahve a test or when labs are due and I am scrolling in bed that is expensive and useless. When the same room has a clear desk, I finish more. That is the only honest version of this cure I can practice before I have a salary.

6. Insure a future income

The sixth cure looks to the future, Arkad talks about providing for the years when you cannot work, and for a family. I am far from that right now. If all I own is this semester then next year I start from zero again. Finishing the degree with marks I can stand behind is the insurance I can buy with my time. For example A simplep ortoflio site pushed to github for a lab will not feed me in ten years by itself. It is a record that I did the work early, so later income has something to stand on when I start my career I do not have dependents right now but I do have a future version of me who will want options so im leaving him nothing but success for his future

7. Increase thy ability to earn

The last cure is the one that fits one of my programming courses(OOP). Arkad says that the more you know, the more you can earn, and that studying a craft is not a side idea because the man who works harder at the same skill will not be stuck at the same pay forever. So thats why I practice, I finish the labs instead of half-doing them. I keep the codes in one organised folder, even when the workload is huge. My desire to be paid is not the skill, the skill is the thing someone can test at any time If I only collect quotes from this book and never get better at the course I am in, I read a chapter and ignored the cure that was aimed for me.

Five laws of gold

The laws come from a different story in the same book. So a father gave his son tablets of gold and five laws and the son lsot the gold because he knew the laws and did not follow them. That part hit me harder than the list itself. That means for me that I can write the rules on this page and still spend the tenth.

Law 1. Gold comes to the person who saves

The first law says that gold comes gladly and in a growing amount to the person who puts aside at least a tenth so there is an estate later. “Estate” is a big word for what I have rn but the law still fits. If I spend everything now I wont have anything to spend on a day that really needs money. something small like, a print, a trip, a water or food. Saving is how the next coin has a place to land.

Law 2. Gold works for a wise owner

The second law says gold works hard for the owner who gives it a profitable job, and it multiplies the way flocks do. Money in a draw or under your bed does not do that. Money pointed at something I understand can though, for me the profitable job is still mostly learning that turns into work: a skill I can repeat. I will not throw the saved tenth at a thing I cannot describe just because the second law says “make it work.”

Law 3. Gold stays with the careful owner

The third law says that gold clings to the careful person who invests with advice from people who actually know how to handle it. RIght now I do not have a banker giving me advice but I do have people who have already done the thing or things that I am trying to do. For money it means I ask myself before I buy a something that is impulsive and I check who the advice is coming from. A stranger with a screenshot is not “men wise in its handling.”

Law 4. Gold slips from what you do not know

The fourth law is the one Im glad i didnt skip. Gold slips away from the person who puts it into a business he is not familiar with or one that skilled people would not approve. The son in his story lost the gold this way. He wanted the profit but he didnt know the work. If someone offered me me a return and I cant explain the work behind it, the law says the gold is already leaving. SO for me I would rather put a little money into something plain that I understand and know it works, even transport and tools for school, than chase a story. Class first, then a skill, then a job. That order is kinda slow but it is the order I can defend.

Law 5. Gold runs from impossible returns

The fifth law says gold flees the person who tries to force it into impossible earnings, or who follows tricksters, or who trusts his own inexperience and his romantic ideas about investing. “Romantic” is the word that i think fits me. It is easy to picture a big result from a small move just because I want the result. The book is pretty direct, you cannot bully gold into coming faster than your work allows. A quick get-rich plan or get rich quick scheme that needs me to skip understanding is the plan the law is describing. I would rather be annoyed that it is slow than be the person who lost the little he had because the offer sounded enticing

Biggest takeaway

I thought that the book was going to be about a rich man telling poor men to try harder and stay consistent or something. But It is was more than that, and also simpler. The main idea was to keep a tenth, do not let your wants grow just because you can name them and think about them on spiot. Do not give money to a plan you cannot explain. Get better at the work so you can earn more later. The lines I remember most is from the laws more than the cures. Knowing the rule is not the same as following it. Because in his story, the son had the tablets and still came home with nothing. I can write all twelve ideas on this page and still spend the data and taxi money on something I do not need tonight but want at a specific time when it comes. So the takeaway I am actually using is small enough to do this month, a tenth goes aside when money comes in. I should put school costs before extras and avoid at all costs a “quick” plan I cannot explain out loud and I keep it consistent even if its slow, because the seventh cure is the one that matches me the best
